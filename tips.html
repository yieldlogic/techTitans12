FARMING TIPS




Grow Better, Harvest Smarter

Great harvests don't happen by accident. Whether you're a first-time grower or a seasoned farmer, these proven tips will help you improve crop health, boost yields, and deliver quality products your buyers will come back for.

01

Test Your Soil Before You Plant

Why it matters: Most yield problems start in the soil. Nutrient deficiencies, wrong pH, and poor drainage are almost always preventable.

  • Test soil 4–6 weeks before planting
  • Check pH, N, P, K, and organic matter
  • Adjust pH before planting — not mid-season
  • Retest every 1–2 years

Pro tip: Log your soil results in Tech Titans — we'll factor them into your yield predictions.

02

Irrigate Based on Soil Moisture, Not the Calendar

Why it matters: Watering on a fixed schedule wastes water, leaches nutrients, and can drown your roots.

  • Check moisture at root depth
  • Water early morning or late afternoon
  • Use drip irrigation where possible (saves up to 50%)
  • Reduce irrigation as crops mature

Pro tip: We'll alert you when soil moisture drops below optimal range.

03

Scout Your Fields Weekly

Why it matters: By the time damage is visible across the field, you've already lost yield. Top farmers walk their fields every week.

  • Scout weekly — same day, same route
  • Check undersides of leaves, stems, soil surface
  • Look for discoloration, holes, wilting, sticky residue
  • Photograph and compare week-to-week
  • Act on thresholds, not panic

Pro tip: Upload photos to Tech Titans and track changes over time.

04

Rotate Crops — Even If It Feels Less Profitable

Why it matters: Planting the same crop year after year drains nutrients, builds up disease, and invites pests.

  • Never plant the same crop family twice in a row
  • Alternate heavy feeders with nitrogen-fixers
  • Use cover crops in the off-season
  • Keep a field history log

Pro tip: Rotation can improve yields by 10–25% over 3 seasons.

05

Know Your Numbers Before Harvest

Why it matters: A great harvest means nothing if costs ate the profit. Track inputs, yield, and price together.

  • Track all input costs per hectare
  • Estimate yield before harvest
  • Monitor market prices weekly
  • Calculate your break-even yield
  • Compare actual vs. predicted each season

Pro tip: Tech Titans shows you estimated revenue and profit before you plant.

Bonus: Keep Records — Even the Boring Ones

"The best farmers I know aren't the ones with the biggest tractors. They're the ones who write things down."
  • Planting dates and varieties
  • Weather events (frost, hail, drought)
  • Every input applied and when
  • Yield and revenue per field
  • What worked and what didn't
